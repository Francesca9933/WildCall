# Welcome to WildCall App 👋

The application is designed to provide community safety, ecological research and personal enrichment. The platform empowers users to interact with the environment responsibly and knowledgeably.
1. SAFETY FIRST: the app functions as a real-time radar, prioritizing not only human safety, but also animals’ safety. We enable tracking of potentially dangerous wildlife sightings in a community-driven approach. Conversely, the platform maps common, harmless animal habitats (e.g., deer, birds, squirrels). This transforms trips into memorable adventures, offering users and especially children the chance to experience close encounters with local fauna. It is particularly useful for people who travel a lot to totally different countries or even continents and don’t know everything about the local wildlife.
2. ECOLOGICAL INSIGHTS: every user is transformed into a citizen scientist since the collected data is invaluable to scientists and experts, providing detailed information on animal movements, including migrations towards urban environments, which is a growing global concern. By monitoring species location and behavior patterns, the application can offer useful data points regarding population health and species at risk of extinction. The platform facilitates free donations to verified green organizations and researchers, providing a direct channel for users to support conservation efforts financially.
3. EDUCATION: users gain access to expert-linked articles, detailed animal descriptions, and interactive games designed to educate the public on biodiversity, and critical environmental topics.
4. DATA MONETIZATION: embedded geographical maps allow us to passively collect aggregate, anonymized data on walkable paths, user habits, and general useful statistics. This structured information represents a further monetization opportunity for developers.
The application adopts a fully responsive design architecture to maximize its utility across all contexts. The content is primarily optimized in a mobile-first approach since it seems the most probable way in which users will dynamically interact with the app, but it is also designed for usage on larger tablet and desktop screens, especially concerning the learning part where people might be comfortably sitting at home, wanting to acquire new knowledge or killing time with instructive games.
